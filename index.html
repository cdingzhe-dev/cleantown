🍌 How to handle food waste

1. Separate it: Keep food scraps in a separate container from plastics, glass and paper. Drain excess liquid.
2. Compost suitable scraps: Fruit and vegetable peels, coffee grounds, tea leaves and eggshells are commonly suitable for home or community composting. Follow the compost service's accepted-items list.
3. Balance and cover: For a home compost bin, mix moist food scraps with dry “browns” such as dry leaves, shredded uncoated cardboard or small twigs. Cover fresh scraps to help deter pests and odours.
4. Avoid common problems: Do not add plastic, packaging or liquids. Meat, fish, dairy, oily food and cooked leftovers can attract pests in basic home composters; only add them if your composting system specifically accepts them.
5. No compost option? Use your local council or community organic-waste collection if available. Otherwise, place food waste in the designated general-waste bin according to local rules—never leave it on the ground or in drains.

Guidance is general. Check the rules used by your local council or recycling provider.
